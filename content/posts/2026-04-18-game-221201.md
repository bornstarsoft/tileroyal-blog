PEARL ABYSS has made waves in the gaming industry with its latest release, 'Crimson Desert,' which has achieved a remarkable milestone of over 5 million copies sold globally in just 26 days. This achievement not only highlights the game's popularity but also marks a significant moment in Korean console gaming history, as it becomes the first title to reach such heights so quickly.

The rapid sales pace of 'Crimson Desert' outstrips even that of last years Game of the Year winner, 'Clair Obscur: Expedition 33,' which took five months to hit the same mark. This impressive feat showcases the growing influence of Korean developers in the global gaming market and sets a new benchmark for future releases.

As we look ahead, it will be fascinating to see how 'Crimson Desert' continues to evolve and what impact this success will have on future titles from PEARL ABYSS and other developers in the industry. Will this set a new standard for upcoming games?
