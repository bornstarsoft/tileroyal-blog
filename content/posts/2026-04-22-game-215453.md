FuRyu, the renowned Japanese developer behind popular JRPGs like The Caligula Effect, has set the gaming community abuzz with a cryptic teaser for a new console game, dubbed Project Alice. The official X account launched on April 22 features an eerie audio clip of a girls voice, oscillating between calm apologies and frantic pleas, hinting at a narrative steeped in angst and possibly horror. The accompanying visuals, initially appearing as a dark void, reveal a haunting image of a reddened, crying eye upon closer inspection.

The anticipation builds as FuRyu promises more clues leading up to a significant announcement on April 25, 2026, at 20:30 JST. This interactive approach invites fans to speculate and piece together the mystery surrounding the game, creating a buzz that could rival some of the biggest reveals in the industry. With a blend of psychological intrigue and horror elements, Project Alice could be a game-changer for FuRyu, potentially expanding their repertoire beyond traditional JRPGs.
