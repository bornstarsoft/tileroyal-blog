With its latest update, Nintendo has significantly enhanced the Virtual Boy game offerings on its Nintendo Switch Online + Expansion Pack service. Five new titles, including classics like V-Tetris and Jack Bros., have been added, bringing the total to 12 games available in the West. This move not only revives nostalgia for fans of the original Virtual Boy console but also showcases Nintendo's commitment to expanding its retro gaming catalog.

The addition of these titles is particularly noteworthy considering that only 22 games were ever released for the Virtual Boy during its brief lifespan in the 1990s. As Nintendo confirms plans for a total of 16 games on the service, players are eagerly anticipating the remaining titles, including fan favorites like Mario Clash and previously unreleased gems like Zero Racer. Will this push for retro gaming inspire a new generation of players to explore the unique charm of the Virtual Boy?
