The Daily Mail has just launched Relink, an innovative puzzle game that promises to challenge your lateral thinking and ignite those lightbulb moments. This daily brainteaser presents players with a grid of 16 words or phrases, where the goal is to identify the four imposters that are cleverly linked by a common theme. It's not just about spotting the odd one out; it's about unraveling the connection that ties them together.

Each day, players get four lives to solve the puzzle, making strategic choices crucial. With a new Relink puzzle dropping at midnight every day, its the perfect way to kickstart your morning or unwind in the evening. Plus, competing with friends to see who can solve it first adds an exciting social element to the challenge.

As we embrace this new era of interactive puzzles, Relink stands out as a testament to how games can stimulate our minds while providing entertainment. Will you take on the challenge and keep your streak alive?
