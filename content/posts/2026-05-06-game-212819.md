In a recent conversation with GamesRadar, Alix Wilton Regan, the new voice of Lara Croft in the upcoming Tomb Raider games, emphasized the importance of recognizing video game actors for their contributions. Regan highlighted that video game performances are often demanding and filled with emotional depth, deserving of acknowledgment just like traditional acting roles in film and theater.

Regan's comments resonate particularly in light of the success of Baldur's Gate 3, which has inspired many in the industry to appreciate the value of their craft. She believes that the recognition of video game actors can elevate the entire medium, enhancing the storytelling and immersive experiences that players cherish. As the industry evolves, will we see more awards and accolades specifically for these talented performers?
