Get ready to embark on a whimsical journey with "Quack Quack Attack - PEGG Blaster," a new mobile puzzle game that promises to captivate players with its unique gameplay and charming characters. Set to launch later this year on iOS and Android, this game allows players to take on the role of Captain Quack, navigating a magical ark while battling monsters and overcoming obstacles in a vibrant, oceanic world.

What sets Quack Quack Attack apart is its innovative gameplay mechanics, where players must strategically select from a roster of up to fifty unique birds, each belonging to one of six classes. This adds a layer of depth and strategy, as players can experiment with different combinations to optimize their approach on each level. The game also features various modes, including the challenging Purgatory Tower and competitive Starry Expedition, ensuring that players have plenty of content to explore.

With pre-registration now open, players can look forward to exciting rewards upon launch, including exclusive in-game items and characters. As the gaming landscape continues to evolve, Quack Quack Attack - PEGG Blaster is poised to make a splash in the mobile gaming market. Will you join Captain Quack on this adventure?
